- 2026-10-23, Task 4 (#53): `lib/replay.mjs` replays methods S, F, R(K), FR, and CR(K) for each species pool, with the quota and the usual-look swap at difficulty step 1 (choice 12). `judgeSteps` runs `cellSteps` on the photos a method judged, so the thin-cell rule fires on those photos only; a key photo in another part is a reject with line `part_error`. A ranked method keeps the joined order of choice 8 inside each of its difficulty steps; photos the ranker left out come after, in fetch order, and a cell with 2 or more photos to rank and no ranking keeps fetch order and counts as "no ranking". A ranking call costs `a + b × n` per cell. `lib/score.mjs` has the scores, the batch-size score, the ranking cost line (least squares; one value of n gives a line through 0), and the decision rule (`gates`, `shortlist`, `candidate`, `heldoutCheck`). `lib/views.mjs` keys a ranking job with `method: 'FR'` under `<model>:<effort>:FR`; a cell with no FR ranking reuses R's (choice 10). `analyze.mjs --set key [--runs a,b] [--repeat run]` writes `tables/key/table.json` and `table.md`; on the key, a species pool interleaves its cells by the key number `n`, and each set-up is scored only on the photos it judged. F and FR have no rows with Haiku as the main judge (choice 10). A cell with no FR ranking reuses R's ranking only when its FR set under rule K3 equals R's ranked set (choice 10); otherwise it counts as "no ranking". Until Task 7 freezes a variant and a rule, the shortlist is provisional and is made per variant and rule (choice 16). R and CR rows at K = 20 and 30 show how often the top photo of "all" comes after photo K. The tracer table in `tables/key/` is not committed: Task 5 commits `tables/key/` with its runs.
