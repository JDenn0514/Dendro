# Leaf-only first version

Date: 2026-09-30. Status: decided by the owner, not yet in the app.

## Decision

The first version of Dendro teaches leaves only. Bark, fruit, and tree form come in later versions.

## Why

- Leaves are where the photos are. A random draw of 180 candidate photos (label round 3, 2026-09-30) gave 106 leaf, 39 bark, and 35 fruit.
- Bark and fruit ran thin in every content run. In run `simple_lobed_us`, 8 of 39 targets reached 6 good photos in all three channels.
- The owner finds bark the hardest channel to judge, and cannot judge the stage of a fruit.

## What follows from it

- **Published bark and fruit photos stay published.** The 548 bark rows and 425 fruit rows in `content/images/manifest.json` are not retired. The app hides them. A later version turns them back on with no new work.
- **New content runs use `--channels leaf`.** A run collects, judges, and publishes leaf photos only.
- **Confusion edges.** Edges on bark and fruit stay in `content/confusion.json`. The app uses only edges on an active channel. Species pairs with no leaf edge get a leaf edge where a source gives a leaf difference.
- **No mention in the app.** The app does not say that bark and fruit come later.
- **The photo-judge eval is leaf only.** It tests leaf verdicts against the owner's leaf labels.

## How to undo it

Add `bark` and `fruit` back to the active channels in the app. No content changes are needed.
